02 · Projects

Projects as engineering cases

Each case presents the original problem, the chosen design, and a verifiable outcome.

.NET backend · payments · subscriptions · recovery

VpnMediator

Payment, subscription, and actual access can diverge after duplicate webhooks, timeouts, or process restarts.

Architecture case study ↗
  • ProblemPayment, subscription, and actual access can diverge after duplicate webhooks, timeouts, or process restarts.
  • SolutionExplicit access and payment states, idempotent operations, audit trails, background reconciliation, migrations, and verifiable recovery paths.
  • ResultA production service with controlled state transitions, backup/restore, health gates, and safe rollback; the public case study excludes secrets and user data.
.NET backend · roles · ledger · payment workflows

CompilationLabLMS

Problem: Learning roles, balances, and payment operations must preserve invariants across retries, migrations, and partial failures.

Solution: Explicit roles and states, a balance ledger, verified webhooks, idempotent payment completion, and critical-flow gates.

Result: The architecture separates user workflows from financial invariants and supports safe migration and rollback.

Architecture case study ↗
.NET · language platform · runtime

UniversalToolchain / Wist2

Problem: Independent language packages must compose into one runtime safely and deterministically without hidden registration-order dependencies.

Solution: Typed artifact contracts, capability/conflict graphs, deterministic routes and passes, exact manifest binding, lock identity, and lifecycle ownership.

Result: 1,465/1,465 tests with zero failures across nine packages and clean-consumer projects, plus interpreter/CIL parity and reproducible configuration checks.
