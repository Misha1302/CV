.NET backend · payments · subscriptions · recovery

VpnMediator

Problem: Payment, subscription, and actual access can diverge after duplicate webhooks, timeouts, or process restarts.

Solution: Explicit access and payment states, idempotent operations, audit trails, background reconciliation, migrations, and verifiable recovery paths.

Result: A production service with controlled state transitions, backup/restore, health gates, and safe rollback; the public case study excludes secrets and user data.

Architecture case study ↗
.NET · modular compiler/runtime framework

UniversalToolchain

Problem: Independent language components must compose into a compiler/runtime pipeline deterministically, without hidden registration-order dependencies.

Solution: Typed intermediate artifacts, deterministic LanguagePlan construction, contribution dependencies/conflicts, pass ordering, and exact backend routing.

Result: The modular framework separates language composition from runtime execution and backs its contracts with executable verification/tests.
