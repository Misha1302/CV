Python · web/Android · offline-first · reliable synchronization

botay! / student-execution-os

Problem: Imported evidence, local edits, and LLM proposals can conflict; disconnected clients must not lose or silently overwrite user intent.

Solution: Separated external observations, canonical state, and derived plans; client operation IDs and atomic server records make offline replay idempotent. Feasibility requires validated witnesses and preserves UNKNOWN where proof is missing.

Result: Browser and native Android clients, migrations and device/release checks; the signed Android 0.8.3 release workflow passed on 10 Oct 2026. External adoption has not been established.

Architecture case study ↗GitHub ↗
.NET backend · payments · subscriptions · recovery

VpnMediator

Problem: Payment, subscription, and actual access can diverge after duplicate webhooks, timeouts, or process restarts.

Solution: Explicit access and payment states, idempotent operations, audit trails, background reconciliation, migrations, and verifiable recovery paths.

Result: Verifiable state transitions, backup/restore, health gates, and rollback-aware deployment; the public case study excludes secrets and user data.
