C++23 · LLVM 22 · interprocedural analysis · optimization

LLVM Interprocedural Global IV Optimization

Problem: The pass must recognize affine evolution of global IVs across calls and transform IR only when correctness conditions are established.

Solution: APInt affine b + k·x evolution, transitive function/module effects, loop/dominator/must-execute reasoning, and a separate conservative legality phase that rejects unsupported CFG/calls.

Result: A conservative LLVM 22 prototype/MVP with an actual IR transform; 29 positive/negative regression cases cover verifier validity, idempotence, and before/after execution.

Architecture case study ↗GitHub ↗
.NET · compiler/language infrastructure · deterministic composition

UniversalToolchain

Problem: Independently owned language contributions must compose into one compiler/runtime pipeline without hidden registration-order dependencies or a second resolver at runtime.

Solution: Typed artifact contracts; dependency/conflict and capability/provider resolution; deterministic pass ordering and artifact routes compiled into an immutable LanguagePlan before execution.

Result: Current exact test manifest: 1,324/1,324. In a frozen-corpus verifier experiment, B2 detected 32/32 primary and 10/10 challenge operators with 0/100 false positives on valid controls; four post-freeze holdouts were checked separately.
