.NET · compiler/runtime platform · SSA

UniversalToolchain / Wist2

Problem: An extensible language must compose from independent modules without losing deterministic compilation or interpreter/CIL semantic parity.

Solution: A Bytecode/AIR pipeline, callable-first SSA, typed compiler facts/effects, verifier-gated transformations, interpreter/CIL backends, and deterministic module composition.

Result: Semantic verification and backend-parity checks act as fail-closed regression gates, with verification records tied to exact revision/run identities.

Architecture case study ↗GitHub ↗
Rust · IR · register allocation · x86-64

x86-64 Codegen & Register Allocation Lab

Problem: The goal is to go from IR to executable x86-64 and verify that register allocation preserves semantics.

Solution: SSA-like IR, live-interval analysis, linear scan, iced-x86, isolated execution, and differential parity with an IR interpreter.

Result: A measurable pipeline with spill/reload metrics, code size, and reproducible result comparison.
