.NET · language platform · runtime

UniversalToolchain / Wist2

Problem: Independent language packages must compose into one runtime safely and deterministically without hidden registration-order dependencies.

Solution: Typed artifact contracts, capability/conflict graphs, deterministic routes and passes, exact manifest binding, lock identity, and lifecycle ownership.

Result: 1,465/1,465 tests with zero failures across nine packages and clean-consumer projects, plus interpreter/CIL parity and reproducible configuration checks.

Rust · IR · register allocation · x86-64

x86-64 Codegen & Register Allocation Lab

Problem: The goal is to go from IR to executable x86-64 and verify that register allocation preserves semantics.

Solution: SSA-like IR, live-interval analysis, linear scan, iced-x86, isolated execution, and differential parity with an IR interpreter.

Result: A measurable pipeline with spill/reload metrics, code size, and reproducible result comparison.
