Compiler testing · configuration plans

PlanFuzz

The fuzzer generates both programs and compile/run configurations, records a failure fingerprint, rechecks it in a fresh process, and minimizes both the program and configuration.

Failures are classified as confirmed, flaky, inconclusive, or infrastructure-related; confirmed failures retain a reproducible minimized example.
