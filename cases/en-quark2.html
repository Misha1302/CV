01

Problem

Public .NET abstractions can obscure invocation overhead and the fragile boundary around CLR internals.

02

Constraints

CLR internals are unstable; microbenchmarks do not prove general production speedups.

03

Solution

Experiments with DynamicMethod, Reflection.Emit, RuntimeMethodHandle and native function pointers; BenchmarkDotNet comparisons of Invoke, delegates and pointers.

04

Outcome

A reproducible research prototype, not a production optimization or a general CLR performance claim.

05

Verification

BenchmarkDotNet harness and reproducible invocation modes; no production claim.
