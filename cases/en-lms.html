The project code is private. This case study intentionally excludes secrets, personal data, and operational identifiers.
01

Problem

Administrator, teacher, curator and student roles, course entitlements, and one-time/subscription payments must remain consistent under retries and network failures.

02

Constraints

Roles, course entitlements, duplicate orders and T-Bank verification; payment intent alone does not grant access.

03

Solution

I define and review ASP.NET Core/PostgreSQL contracts and T-Bank payment requirements: order idempotency and authoritative provider state checks before entitlement grants. Parts of implementation were produced by AI agents.

04

Outcome

Work in progress: checks and sandbox payments do not establish production readiness. No internal coin-wallet model.

05

Verification

Review of role and entitlement checks, T-Bank sandbox payment verification, migrations and critical flows; no production launch.

06

My ownership

Architecture and acceptance criteria; review of agent-generated roles, entitlements and T-Bank sandbox payments.
