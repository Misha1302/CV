Problem

An extensible language must compose from independent modules without losing deterministic compilation or interpreter/CIL semantic parity.

03

Solution

04

Semantic verification and backend-parity checks act as fail-closed regression gates, with verification records tied to exact revision/run identities.

Canonical test gate, clean-consumer projects, interpreter/CIL parity, deterministic manifests and replayable failures.
