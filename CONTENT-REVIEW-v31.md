# Content review — targeted CV v31

## Decision

PASS for the three flagship variants after clean PDF rebuild and visual review.

## Positioning changes

- Compiler now presents Wist2 as a typed language platform rather than an educational compiler with a list of passes.
- Compiler Tools now presents PlanFuzz as the primary differentiator.
- C++ Systems keeps PS-form/AdvancedAlgorithms/MCST first and uses Wist2 as additional architecture evidence.
- Backend leads with deployed systems and state/recovery evidence rather than an unrelated competition score.
- Reliability is explicitly a backend specialization, not an SRE claim.

## Claim boundaries

- PlanFuzz has no claim of superiority over program-only fuzzing until equal-budget baselines are complete.
- Callable-first SSA is opt-in and partial; no SSA-native backend or performance claim is made.
- Wist compiled execution is not described as a hardened sandbox.
